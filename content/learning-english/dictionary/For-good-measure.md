# For good measure

**音标**: /fɔːr ɡʊd ˈmeʒər/

**词性**: phr.

**释义**: 为了安全起见；额外地；作为补充；好像还嫌不够似的

**例句** (原文): He placed his heavy army-issue boot on the young man's ankle and pressed hard, causing him to fall to the ground and cry out in pain. For good measure he thrust his knee in the boy's face, breaking his eyeglasses.
