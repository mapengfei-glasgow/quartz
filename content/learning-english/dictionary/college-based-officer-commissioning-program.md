# college-based, officer-commissioning program

**音标**: /ˈkɒlɪdʒ ˈbeɪst ˈɒfɪsər kəˈmɪʃənɪŋ ˈproʊɡræm/

**词性**: n. phr.

**释义**: 基于大学的军官任命项目；由大学提供的培训项目，旨在为学生提供军事官员委任资格

**例句** (原文): Looking for answers, he joined the Reserve Officers' Training Corps, the college-based, officer-commissioning program.
