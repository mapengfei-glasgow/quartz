# powerhouse

**音标**: /ˈpaʊərhaʊs/

**词性**: n.

**释义**: 强国；有实力的机构；才华横溢的人；势力强大的团体

**例句** (原文): The aristocratic, Yale-educated Lehman had reigned during the firm's glory years and was a banker to some of the biggest and most important U.S. corporations early in the American Century...turned it into a financial powerhouse in post-Depression America.
