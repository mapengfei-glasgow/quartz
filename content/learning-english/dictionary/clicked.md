# clicked

**音标**: /klɪkt/

**词性**: v. (past tense of click)

**释义**: （与某人）相处融洽；立即成功；顿悟；（通常与"with"连用）产生默契

**例句** (原文): What attracted him was not the fulfillment of some lifelong dream about playing with other people's money, but rather something far more visceral, something that instantly clicked.
